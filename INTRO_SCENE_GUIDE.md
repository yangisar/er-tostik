# Intro Scene: Er Tostik and the Oracle

## Scene Description
**Setting:** Beautiful sunny steppe (Kazakhstan grasslands) at dawn

**Characters:**
1. **Er Tostik** - The hero warrior standing at the edge of a deep chasm
   - Wearing his traditional Central Asian armor
   - Facing the chasm nervously
   
2. **The Oracle** - Mysterious hooded figure 3x taller than Er Tostik
   - Stands on the opposite side of the chasm
   - Wearing flowing dark robes with mystical symbols
   - Face hidden in shadow of hood
   - Glowing eyes visible

**Environment:**
- Golden steppe grass waving in wind
- Bright blue sky with wispy clouds
- Sun rising behind the Oracle (creating dramatic backlight)
- Deep dark chasm between them (entrance to underworld)
- Rocky cliff edges

## Oracle's Message
Speech bubble appears above Oracle saying:
**"To save your brothers, you must descend to the underworld"**

## Player Actions
- Walk right using joystick → Er Tostik walks toward chasm and falls in
- Press JUMP button → Er Tostik jumps into the chasm
- Either action triggers descent into the main game

## Visual Transition
1. Er Tostik falls into darkness
2. Screen fades to black
3. Main game begins (underground tunnel system)

## Audio Cues
- Peaceful steppe wind sounds (if background audio available)
- Dramatic music sting when Oracle speaks
- Descending sound effect when falling into chasm
- Transition to main dark theme music
